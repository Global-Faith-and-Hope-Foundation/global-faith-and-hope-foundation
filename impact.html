Our Impact

Supporting wellbeing, resilience and livelihoods for vulnerable communities, with particular focus on children, women, youth and other disadvantaged groups.

🎓

Education Support

Scholarship initiatives have supported refugee communities around Ndzaleka Camp, helping vulnerable learners access educational opportunities.

👩🏽‍🤝‍👨🏾

Women & Youth Empowerment

GFHF has worked with youth organizations to promote leadership, empowerment and responsible management of natural resources.

🌳

Environmental Conservation

Through work with communities and journalists in Mchinji, GFHF has promoted tree replenishment and environmental conservation.

☀️

Climate Resilience & Livelihoods

GFHF promotes climate-smart agriculture, drought-resistant crops, food security, sustainable livelihoods and fuel-efficient cookstoves.

💧

Health & Community Wellbeing

GFHF works through community structures to promote HIV/AIDS awareness, WASH, cholera prevention, health referrals and social support.

🤝

Community Development

GFHF strengthens community structures and encourages collaboration with local and district authorities to improve community resilience.

Community-based impact

Through community-based programs and partnerships, GFHF has supported initiatives in education, livelihoods, youth empowerment, health awareness, environmental conservation and climate resilience.
