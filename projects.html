Our Projects & Community Engagement

GFHF's experience spans education, youth empowerment, environmental conservation and community development.

2018–2022

Refugee Education & Scholarships

GFHF implemented privately funded scholarship initiatives supporting refugee communities around Ndzaleka Camp. The initiatives were made possible through support from diaspora well-wishers, helping vulnerable learners access educational opportunities.

2022–2023

Youth Empowerment & COVID-19 Awareness

In collaboration with youth organizations in Dowa, GFHF supported COVID-19 awareness and youth empowerment activities, including leadership development and responsible natural resource management.

2023–2024

Environmental Conservation & Tree Replenishment

GFHF worked with journalists and local communities in Mchinji to promote awareness of tree replenishment, environmental conservation, and the importance of sustainable management of natural resources.

Current

Education & Environmental Partnership Development

GFHF has initiated preliminary engagements with communities and schools in high-density urban areas, including Ntandile and Chilimampunga School. These engagements are exploring future collaboration in education support for vulnerable communities and environmental activities and are currently at the pre-positioning and partnership-development stage.
