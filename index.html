Who We Are

Compassion, inclusion and community-led development are at the heart of GFHF.

Global Faith and Hope Foundation (GFHF) is a Malawi-based, community-focused non-governmental organization established in Dowa District to respond to the humanitarian and social needs of vulnerable communities. The organization was founded with a strong commitment to compassion, inclusion and community-led development. GFHF was formally registered with the Malawi NGO Board in 2021 and operates under the strategic oversight of a Board of Trustees.

GFHF’s mandate is to improve the wellbeing, resilience and livelihoods of vulnerable communities, with particular focus on children, women, youth and other disadvantaged groups.

Our Areas of Work

GFHF works across interconnected areas that support people, communities and the environment.

🌱

Climate & Environment

Climate-smart agriculture, tree planting, biodiversity protection, drought-resistant crops and environmental awareness.

🎓

Education & Empowerment

Education support, bursaries, youth empowerment and initiatives that expand opportunities for vulnerable people.

🤝

Health & Community Wellbeing

HIV/AIDS awareness, WASH, cholera prevention, referrals, psychosocial support and stronger community structures.

Addressing climate change through conservation and biodiversity protection

GFHF works through community structures to strengthen resilience to climate risks while supporting vulnerable individuals and encouraging collaboration with local and district authorities.
