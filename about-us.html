Global Faith and Hope Foundation (GFHF)

Global Faith and Hope Foundation (GFHF) is a Malawi-based, community-focused non-governmental organization established in Dowa District to respond to the humanitarian and social needs of vulnerable communities. The organization was founded with a strong commitment to compassion, inclusion and community-led development. GFHF was formally registered with the Malawi NGO Board in 2021 and operates under the strategic oversight of a Board of Trustees.

👁

Vision

To seek a world of hope for all, where discrimination is eliminated and equal opportunities are available to everyone.

🎯

Mission

In partnership with others leverage talent, resources and capacities to local to global influence systems that create opportunities for the vulnerable and marginalized people and their families.
