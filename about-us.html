Who We Are

Global Faith and Hope Foundation (GFHF) is a Malawi-based, community-focused NGO established in Dowa District to respond to humanitarian and social needs of vulnerable communities.

Formally registered with the Malawi NGO Board in 2021 and under the strategic oversight of a Board of Trustees, GFHF seeks to improve wellbeing, resilience and livelihoods, especially for children, women, youth and disadvantaged groups.

Vision

To seek a world of hope for all, where discrimination is eliminated and equal opportunities are available to everyone.

Mission

In partnership with others leverage talent, resources and capacities to local to global influence systems that create opportunities for the vulnerable and marginalized people and their families.
